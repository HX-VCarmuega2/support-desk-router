MERIDIAN CLOUD — INTERNAL FINANCE SUPPORT FAQ KNOWLEDGE BASE
Version: 1.0
Purpose: Internal support reference for employees of the fictional B2B SaaS company Meridian Cloud, covering expenses, payroll, billing, and procurement.

ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT

Meridian Cloud is a fictional mid-size SaaS company. This knowledge base is intended for the internal finance support desk and for automated support agents answering employee and customer questions about expenses, payroll, billing, and procurement policy. Some topics are intentionally similar so that retrieval systems can be tested for relevance and precision.

==================================================
SECTION 1 — EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
==================================================

FAQ 01 — How does an employee submit an expense reimbursement request?

Employees submit expenses through the expense portal, attaching a receipt and selecting the appropriate expense category. Manager approval is required before the request routes to Finance for payment processing.

FAQ 02 — What is the maximum reimbursable amount without pre-approval?

Expenses under $200 do not require pre-approval and can be submitted directly for reimbursement. Expenses of $200 or more require manager pre-approval before the purchase is made, documented in the expense portal.

FAQ 03 — How long does expense reimbursement take to process?

Approved expense reports are reimbursed within 5 to 7 business days via direct deposit to the employee's payroll account. Reports submitted with missing receipts are held until the required documentation is provided.

FAQ 04 — What receipts are required for expense reimbursement?

An itemized receipt is required for any expense over $25, showing the vendor, date, and amount. A credit card statement alone is not sufficient documentation for reimbursement purposes.

FAQ 05 — Are meals during business travel reimbursable, and up to what amount?

Meals during business travel are reimbursable up to $75 per day, itemized by meal, without needing pre-approval as long as the trip itself was approved. Alcohol is reimbursable only up to $25 per day and must be itemized separately.

FAQ 06 — Can personal expenses be reimbursed if mixed with a business trip?

Personal expenses are not reimbursable even when incurred during an approved business trip; only the business-related portion of any mixed expense is eligible. Employees must clearly itemize and separate personal charges when submitting the report.

SECTION 2 — TRAVEL POLICY
==================================================

FAQ 07 — What is the company's policy on booking flights for business travel?

All business flights must be booked through the approved corporate travel platform to ensure duty-of-care tracking and negotiated rates. Booking outside the platform requires an exception approved by the employee's manager in advance.

FAQ 08 — Is business class travel ever approved?

Business class is approved only for flights longer than 6 hours in the air or for employees with a documented medical accommodation. All other domestic and short-haul travel is booked in economy class.

FAQ 09 — How far in advance must business travel be booked?

Travel should be booked at least 14 days in advance whenever possible to secure better fares and availability. Last-minute travel under 14 days requires manager approval noting the business reason for short notice.

FAQ 10 — What is the per-diem rate for international travel?

The international per-diem rate is $85 per day for meals and incidentals, adjusted for high-cost cities such as London, Tokyo, or San Francisco, where the rate increases to $110 per day. Per-diem amounts are published in the finance intranet page by city tier.

FAQ 11 — Who approves travel requests above the standard budget?

Travel exceeding $3,000 in total cost requires department head approval in addition to the direct manager's approval. This includes conference travel packages that bundle airfare, lodging, and registration fees.

SECTION 3 — INVOICING AND CUSTOMER BILLING
==================================================

FAQ 12 — How often are customers invoiced for their subscription?

Customers on monthly plans are invoiced on the same calendar day each month as their initial signup date. Customers on annual plans are invoiced once per year, with a renewal notice sent 30 days before the renewal date.

FAQ 13 — What payment methods does the company accept from customers?

Meridian Cloud accepts credit card and ACH bank transfer for self-serve customers, and additionally accepts wire transfer for enterprise customers on annual contracts. Payment method changes can be made by the customer's billing administrator in the account settings.

FAQ 14 — How does a customer request a billing correction?

Customers submit billing correction requests through the in-app support widget or by emailing the billing support alias, including the invoice number and description of the discrepancy. Corrections are reviewed and resolved within 5 business days.

FAQ 15 — What is the grace period before a subscription is suspended for non-payment?

Accounts with a failed payment enter a 14-day grace period during which service continues while the customer resolves the payment issue. If payment is not resolved within the grace period, the account is downgraded to a restricted read-only state.

FAQ 16 — How are annual subscription plans billed compared to monthly plans?

Annual plans are billed as a single upfront payment for the full year, at a 15% discount compared to paying monthly. Monthly plans are billed in recurring installments with no long-term commitment and can be canceled at any time.

FAQ 17 — Can a customer request a refund after canceling their subscription?

Monthly plan cancellations are not refunded for the current billing period, but access continues until the period ends. Annual plans canceled within the first 30 days are eligible for a prorated refund; cancellations after 30 days are not refunded.

SECTION 4 — PAYROLL
==================================================

FAQ 18 — What is the company's payroll schedule?

Employees are paid on a semi-monthly schedule, on the 15th and last business day of each month. If a pay date falls on a weekend or holiday, payment is issued on the preceding business day.

FAQ 19 — How does an employee set up or change direct deposit?

Direct deposit is set up or updated through the payroll portal by entering bank routing and account numbers. Changes submitted before the payroll cutoff, typically 5 business days before pay date, take effect in the next paycheck.

FAQ 20 — When are annual tax forms (W-2 equivalents) issued to employees?

Annual tax documents are issued electronically through the payroll portal by January 31 each year, with a physical copy available on request. Employees can access prior years' tax documents through the same portal for record-keeping.

FAQ 21 — How are payroll discrepancies corrected?

Employees report payroll discrepancies to the Payroll team within 5 business days of the pay date for fastest resolution. Corrections identified before the next payroll cutoff are included in the following pay cycle; urgent cases may be issued as an off-cycle payment.

FAQ 22 — Are bonuses paid separately from regular payroll?

Performance and referral bonuses are paid as a separate off-cycle payment rather than combined with regular salary, typically within 30 days of approval. Bonus payments are subject to a different, often higher, default tax withholding rate.

SECTION 5 — PROCUREMENT AND PURCHASE ORDERS
==================================================

FAQ 23 — How does a team request a purchase order for a new vendor?

Purchase order requests are submitted through the procurement portal, including vendor details, cost, and business justification. New vendors must complete a vendor onboarding and security review before a purchase order can be issued.

FAQ 24 — What spending threshold requires manager approval before purchasing?

Purchases of $500 or more require direct manager approval before a purchase order is issued. Purchases under $500 can be made using a corporate card without additional approval, subject to standard expense policy.

FAQ 25 — What spending threshold requires finance department approval?

Purchases exceeding $10,000 require additional approval from the Finance department beyond the standard manager approval, including a review of budget availability. Purchases exceeding $50,000 also require CFO sign-off.

FAQ 26 — How long does vendor onboarding take before a purchase order is issued?

Standard vendor onboarding, including security and data privacy review, takes approximately 10 business days. Vendors handling customer data or requiring system integration may take longer due to additional security assessment steps.

FAQ 27 — Can an employee make a purchase without a purchase order in an emergency?

Emergency purchases under $1,000 can be made on a corporate card with manager notification, followed by a retroactive purchase order filed within 3 business days. Purchases above $1,000 require verbal approval from Finance before the purchase, even in an emergency.

SECTION 6 — VENDOR PAYMENTS
==================================================

FAQ 28 — What is the standard payment term offered to vendors?

Standard payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date, unless otherwise negotiated in the vendor contract. Preferred vendors with long-standing relationships may be offered net 45 terms upon request.

FAQ 29 — How does a vendor submit an invoice for payment?

Vendors submit invoices to the dedicated accounts payable email address, referencing the associated purchase order number. Invoices without a valid purchase order number are held for review and may delay payment.

FAQ 30 — What happens if a vendor invoice is disputed?

Disputed invoices are flagged in the accounts payable system and the requesting department is notified to resolve the discrepancy with the vendor. Payment is held until the dispute is resolved or a corrected invoice is submitted.

FAQ 31 — How are recurring vendor subscriptions tracked and renewed?

Recurring vendor subscriptions are tracked in the procurement system with automated renewal alerts sent to the owning department 60 days before renewal. Department owners must confirm continued need or the subscription is flagged for cancellation review.

SECTION 7 — BUDGET AND APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
==================================================

FAQ 32 — How are department budgets allocated each fiscal year?

Department budgets are proposed by department heads and approved by Finance and executive leadership during annual planning in Q4, based on headcount plans and strategic priorities. Approved budgets are loaded into the finance system at the start of the fiscal year.

FAQ 33 — What happens if a department exceeds its quarterly budget?

Departments exceeding their quarterly budget by more than 10% must submit a variance explanation to Finance and may face a spending freeze on non-essential purchases for the remainder of the quarter.

FAQ 34 — Who approves budget reallocation requests mid-year?

Mid-year budget reallocation between categories within the same department requires department head approval. Reallocation between different departments additionally requires Finance approval to ensure overall budget integrity.

FAQ 35 — How often are budget reports shared with department heads?

Finance distributes budget-to-actual reports to department heads monthly, along with a more detailed quarterly review meeting to discuss variances and forecasts for the remainder of the year.

SECTION 8 — TAX FORMS AND DOCUMENTS
==================================================

FAQ 36 — How does an employee update their tax withholding elections?

Withholding elections are updated directly in the payroll portal at any time, with changes reflected in the next payroll cycle if submitted before the cutoff. Employees are encouraged to review elections annually or after major life events.

FAQ 37 — Where can an employee access past pay stubs and tax documents?

Pay stubs and tax documents are available in the payroll portal under the documents section, with history retained for at least 7 years for compliance purposes. Former employees retain portal access for tax document retrieval for 3 years after departure.

FAQ 38 — Does the company provide tax filing assistance?

Meridian Cloud does not provide individual tax filing assistance, but offers a discounted subscription to a third-party tax preparation service as an optional employee perk. Employees with equity compensation are encouraged to consult a tax professional.

FAQ 39 — How are relocation expenses reported for tax purposes?

Relocation reimbursements are reported as taxable income per current federal guidelines and appear on the employee's annual tax form. Finance provides a summary of relocation-related taxable amounts alongside the year-end tax documents.

SECTION 9 — CORPORATE CARDS
==================================================

FAQ 40 — How does an employee request a corporate card?

Corporate card requests are submitted through the finance portal with manager approval, and cards are typically issued within 5 business days. Cards are tied to the employee's expense profile and default spending limit for their role.

FAQ 41 — What is the monthly spending limit on a standard corporate card?

Standard corporate cards have a default monthly limit of $2,500, which can be increased temporarily for specific business needs, such as event planning, with manager and Finance approval.

FAQ 42 — What purchases are prohibited on a corporate card?

Corporate cards cannot be used for personal purchases, cash advances, or purchases from unapproved vendor categories such as gambling. Any prohibited transaction identified during reconciliation must be reimbursed by the employee immediately.

FAQ 43 — What happens if a corporate card charge is not reconciled on time?

Unreconciled charges after 30 days trigger an automated reminder, and after 60 days the card may be temporarily suspended until outstanding reconciliations are completed. Repeated late reconciliation may result in card privileges being revoked.

FAQ 44 — How is a lost or stolen corporate card reported?

Employees report a lost or stolen corporate card immediately to the card issuer's hotline to freeze the account, then notify Finance so a replacement card can be issued. Any unauthorized charges identified are disputed directly with the card issuer.

SECTION 10 — ADDITIONAL FINANCE TOPICS
==================================================

FAQ 45 — How does the finance team handle multi-currency expense reports?

Expenses incurred in foreign currency are converted to the employee's local payroll currency using the exchange rate on the date of the transaction, sourced from the expense platform's built-in currency conversion tool.

FAQ 46 — What is the process for requesting an advance on reimbursement for a large trip?

Employees expecting a trip cost above $2,000 can request a travel advance through the finance portal, disbursed up to 5 business days before departure. The advance is reconciled against the actual expense report submitted after the trip.

FAQ 47 — How are equity/stock option grants reported to employees?

Equity grants are documented in the equity management platform, which employees can access to view vesting schedules and exercise history. Tax implications of vesting or exercising options are summarized annually but individual tax advice is not provided.

FAQ 48 — What is the policy on client gifts and entertainment expenses?

Client gifts and entertainment are reimbursable up to $150 per client per year, requiring the client's name and business purpose to be documented in the expense report. Gifts exceeding this threshold require Finance pre-approval.

FAQ 49 — How does a customer update their billing contact or payment method?

Customers update their billing contact and payment method directly in the account billing settings, accessible to users with the billing administrator role. Enterprise customers with a dedicated account manager can also request changes through their account manager.

FAQ 50 — What is the dispute process for a declined customer payment?

When a customer payment is declined, an automated retry occurs after 3 days, followed by an email notification to the billing contact. If payment continues to fail, the standard 14-day grace period applies before service restrictions begin.

FAQ 51 — How are enterprise customer contracts invoiced differently from self-serve customers?

Enterprise customers are invoiced according to custom contract terms negotiated during the sales process, which may include net 30 or net 60 payment terms rather than automatic card billing. Self-serve customers are billed automatically via the payment method on file.

FAQ 52 — What is the year-end close process for the finance team?

Year-end close involves reconciling all accounts, finalizing revenue recognition for the fiscal year, and preparing financial statements for audit, typically completed within 3 weeks after fiscal year end. Department heads must submit final expense reports before the close deadline.

FAQ 53 — How does an employee request reimbursement for a home internet stipend?

The monthly home internet stipend for remote employees is processed automatically through payroll rather than the expense portal, based on remote work status recorded in Workday, and does not require a separate reimbursement request.

FAQ 54 — What audit documentation must be retained for expense reports?

Employees must retain original receipts for expenses over $25 for at least 1 year in case of an audit, even though the expense portal stores a digital copy. Finance may request original documentation during periodic internal audits.
